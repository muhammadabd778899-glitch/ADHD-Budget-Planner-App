# Etsy / Public Listing Copy

## Short description

A calm, local-first budget planner for people who want one clear number: how much is safe to spend.

## Longer description

Tide helps you plan money without shame, spreadsheet fatigue, or hidden assumptions. It keeps your
budget in your own browser, calculates safe to spend from your own records, and separates bills,
savings, debt, and expected income clearly.

Perfect for ADHD brains that need a low-friction, forgiving, and private way to answer: “How much
can I spend before things get hard?”

## Marketing claims that are supported

- Local-first, offline-capable planner.
- One safe-to-spend number with a documented formula.
- Expected income kept separate from received income.
- Backups and CSV exports for your own records.

## Claims that are not supported

Tide is not a bank, financial advisor, investment tool, tax tool, or lending product. It does not
connect to accounts and cannot guarantee affordability or an outcome.
